  python3 rotate_model_to_gif.py --frames 60 --fps 20

X-axis spin (front view rotation):
  python3 rotate_model_to_gif.py --axis x --frames 48 --fps 15

  python3 rotate_model_to_gif.py --output custom_location/animation.gif

Process different model:
  python3 rotate_model_to_gif.py --obj 3DModel_2/OBJ/3DModel.obj

Combine multiple options:
  python3 rotate_model_to_gif.py --axis y --frames 72 --fps 24 --output 3DModel_1/high_quality.gif
